| 中断后观察 | 结果 | 证据 |
|---|---|---|
| profile `package.json`、`pnpm-lock.yaml` | 已指向 rc.5 | [中断后核对](../attachments/public-validation.md) |
| 实际 `node_modules/dsh-auto-memory-wh` | 仍为 rc.4，旧 bundle 哈希 `653138E3E7D8EDF7CE9007E1C9A2BC5BC45ECB36FCC42F2DFE8A8CAE3A164D87` | [同上](../attachments/public-validation.md) |
